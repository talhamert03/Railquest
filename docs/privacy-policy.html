1. Who we are

Railquest is a puzzle game published on Google Play by AlphaWolf Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play Railquest and why.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not use analytics tools.

3. Data stored on your device

Your level progress, settings (sound, music, haptics and similar), selected wagon design and a record of your purchases are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Railquest shows ads through Google AdMob: an interstitial ad after some completed levels and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (views, taps, completed videos) and diagnostic data.

This data is used for showing ads, limiting how often you see them, preventing fraud and measuring performance. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Railquest asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

“No Ads” and the wagon designs are sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives which products your Google account owns so it can unlock them. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services described above.

7. Children

Railquest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Communication between the app and Google’s ad and billing services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, you can exercise these rights for ad data directly with Google, change your ad consent in the game, or contact us for help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

3. Cihazında saklanan veriler

Bölüm ilerlemen, ayarların (ses, müzik, titreşim vb.), seçtiğin vagon tasarımı ve satın alımlarının kaydı yalnızca cihazında saklanır. Bu veriler bize gönderilmez. Uygulamayı kaldırdığında veya verilerini temizlediğinde silinir.

4. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Railquest, Google AdMob üzerinden reklam gösterir: bazı bölümlerin ardından geçiş reklamı ve kendi isteğinle izlediğin ödüllü videolar. Reklamları göstermek ve ölçmek için Google şu verileri toplayıp işleyebilir:

  • cihazının reklam kimliği,
  • IP adresi ve buna dayalı yaklaşık konum,
  • cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, uygulama sürümü),
  • reklam etkileşimleri (görüntüleme, dokunma, tamamlanan videolar) ve tanılama verileri.

Bu veriler reklam göstermek, gösterim sıklığını sınırlamak, sahtekârlığı önlemek ve performansı ölçmek için kullanılır. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre’de kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlardan önce Google’ın onay formuyla iznin istenir; tercihini oyunda Ayarlar → Gizlilik bölümünden istediğin zaman değiştirebilirsin. Her Android cihazda reklam kimliğini sistem ayarlarından (Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar) sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin.

Ayrıntılar: Google, hizmetlerini kullanan uygulamalardaki bilgileri nasıl kullanır ve Google Gizlilik Politikası.
